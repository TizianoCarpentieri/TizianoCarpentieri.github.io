How to use it

One chat to clarify.One to work.

SocraticSelf works inside your AI agent, with your project open. It gives you back the clear request, and you take it to a new chat in the same project.

Open your agent in your project.

Write it the way it comes.

It reads the files. If needed, it asks.

It gives the request back, clear.

Paste it into a new chat, same project.

How it works

Four moves.Every time.

It looks.Reads your project and names the real files.
It asks.Three plain questions at most. Only if needed.
It speaks as you.First person. Your language. Your trade's words.
It shows the gaps.What you still don't know. What you might be missing.
